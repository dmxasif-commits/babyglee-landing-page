FAQ

Quick answers

How do I order?

Choose a size for each colour you like, add them to your cart, then check out once with your delivery details. We call you to confirm your sizes and delivery before shipping.

Can I buy more than one pair or more than one colour?

Yes. Add as many pairs, colours and sizes as you like to the cart and pay for them in a single checkout.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available on every order. You pay in taka when your parcel arrives.

What if the size doesn't fit?

You can exchange within 72 hours of delivery. Delivery and return charges apply. See our Shipping & Returns page.

Which sizes do you have?

The Bounce series comes in EU sizes 21 to 36, suitable for roughly ages 1 to 10. Use the size guide to match your child's foot length.

Are the shoes safe for kids?

They are made from microfiber synthetic leather and breathable mesh, with chemical limits for REACH, phthalates and azo dyes.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver to homes across Bangladesh, inside and outside Dhaka.
