FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

What is Babyglee?

Babyglee is your destination for high-quality, comfortable and stylish shoes for children aged 1 to 10 years. We focus on durable, safe and supportive footwear for all occasions.

How do I find the right shoe size?

Use our size guide: measure your child's foot, type the length in cm and we suggest the size. The insole should be your child's foot length + 1 cm (shown in cm and inches in the size table). For wide feet or thick socks, go one size up. We also confirm the size with you by call or message before shipping.

Which sizes do you have?

The Bounce series comes in EU sizes 21 to 36, suitable for roughly ages 1 to 10.

How do I place an order?

Choose a size for each colour you like, add them to your cart, then check out once with your delivery details. We call you to confirm your sizes and delivery before shipping.

Where do you deliver from, and how long does it take?

Our head office is in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and we deliver to homes across Bangladesh, inside and outside Dhaka. Delivery takes 1–3 days everywhere.

What are the shipping costs?

The delivery charge for inside Dhaka and outside Dhaka is shown at checkout before you confirm your order. Full details are on our Shipping & Returns page.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order is dispatched, the courier gives you a tracking number, and you can use it on the courier's website for live updates on your delivery.

Can I pay on delivery? Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery is available on every order: you pay in taka when your parcel arrives. You can also pay by bKash (merchant account 01974974973, Payment option). Tell our team when we call to confirm your order.

What is your return and exchange policy?

You can exchange within 72 hours of receiving your shoes, for items in original condition. Delivery and return charges apply. See our Shipping & Returns page for details.

How can I contact Babyglee customer service?

Reach us on WhatsApp +880 1974-974973 (11 AM – 9 PM BST), on Facebook Messenger (Babyglee.Official) or by email at info@babygleebd.com. Our head office in Dhaka, Bangladesh, manages all customer enquiries.
