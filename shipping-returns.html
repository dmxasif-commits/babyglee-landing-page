Shipping & returns policy

Shipping Policy

At Babyglee, we ensure quick, reliable and hassle-free delivery for your child's new shoes. Prices are in Bangladeshi taka (৳ BDT).

How we deliver

  • Fast processing: orders are processed from our Dhaka, Bangladesh office within 1 business day. Our team calls or messages you to confirm your child's size and your delivery address before shipping.
  • Delivery time: 2–3 days everywhere in Bangladesh, to your home.
  • Clear costs: the delivery charge for inside Dhaka and outside Dhaka is shown at checkout before you confirm your order.
  • Easy tracking: once your order is dispatched, the courier gives you a tracking number for live updates.
  • Please keep your phone available so the courier can reach you.

Payment

Cash on Delivery is available on every order: you pay the courier when you receive your parcel. Please check the parcel before paying. You can also pay by bKash (merchant account 01974974973, Payment option).

Return & Exchange Policy

Your satisfaction is key! We make exchanges simple at Babyglee.

  • Eligibility: exchange items within 72 hours of receipt. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with all tags and in the original packaging.
  • How to start: message us on WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger, or email info@babygleebd.com with your order number.
  • Charges: delivery and return charges apply to exchanges, unless the error is ours.
  • Refunds: where a refund applies, it is issued to your original payment method within 7–10 business days after inspection.
  • Damaged or defective: contact us immediately with photos and your order details for a quick resolution.
